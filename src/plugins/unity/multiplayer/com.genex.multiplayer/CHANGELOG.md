# Changelog

All notable changes to `com.genex.multiplayer`. Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org); before 1.0 a minor bump may add API.

## Unreleased

- **Your shots on UDP are confirmed, not counted refused (PROTOCOL.md §17.12 *Client*, §17.9; lane UP).** A fast seat settled a
  projectile prediction against the newest snapshot's ack, which runs ahead of the WebSocket that carries the launch; it now
  settles against the ack of the newest snapshot at or before the WebSocket's tick (`GenexSettleAckTrail`, the `settleAck` vectors).
- **`covered` asks only the solids that can answer (PROTOCOL.md §17.7 *Starting inside*).** A body inside a solid costs a step the same beside 30,000 far solids; a fine bake's walk no longer scales with the world. Answers are unchanged, held by `firstperson.json`.
- **A large world costs a contact query the solids near it (PROTOCOL.md §17.7, §17.11 *Contact policy*).** The contact world now holds the TypeScript's grid, and a cast, the
  push-out and its covered test visit a cell's solids rather than every solid. Answers are unchanged, held by `contact-policy.json`'s `points`.
- **A level to a world (`WORLD-SCENE.md`; lane AD-UNITY).** `Genex > Export World Scene...` and `GenexWorldExporter.RunBatch` walk a scene's Box, Sphere, Capsule, Mesh and Terrain colliders into the world scene `genex world fit` reads: moving ones named and not baked, mirrored ones read as PhysX reads them, layers mapped to channels, bad values refused by name with no file. New marks `GenexWorldActor` and `GenexWorldSpawn`. The package now depends on the physics, terrain, terrainphysics and animation modules (a build strips what it does not use). Rules held by `GenexSmoke world-export`, the walk and the physics comparison by `Tests~/WorldExport`.
- **A step up works in any game's units (PROTOCOL.md §17.7, `firstperson` and `vehicle`).** A step had to beat the flat slide by a fixed amount in world units, so a game in metres needed about 3.8 m/s to climb a kerb, and could not climb one from rest or at a walk. The amount, friction's stop and the speed that counts as rising now follow §17.7's `unit`: a hull under half its preset's default radius is a game in other units and they scale with it. Every room at or over half the default radius (car-ball's 55 too) moves bit for bit as before.
- **UDP by default (PROTOCOL.md §17.12; lane UD).** `GenexAuthorityOptions.Fast` is true: a seat asks for `fast@1` beside `tick@1` unless the game sets it false, and falls back to the WebSocket by itself. A WebGL player never claims it (no sockets). One log line per join names the transport and any fallback (`GenexFastStatus.Line`, `Transport`), held to the new `transportLine` vectors. Behaviour change for a game that never set it.
- **A hitch costs a held stick no correction (PROTOCOL.md §17.11 *Input pacing*; lane FLAKE5).** While the state is
  stepped past the last input you sent (a frame hitch drained your seat's buffer and the room repeated for inputs not
  sent yet) the reconcile waits at the input it compared at last (`GenexInputRepeat.ReconcileThrough`) instead of comparing at the last
  input sent, which pulled your entity forward by the repeats and back by as many. Held to the new `reconciles` vectors.
- **Fast inputs carry their render time; bodies a fast snapshot lacks stay out of the keyframe (PROTOCOL.md §17.12
  *Render-time stamp*, *Client*; lane M4c).** WIRE CHANGE of `fast@1`: the uplink header is 29 bytes (`render` uint32),
  each action carries `render` uint32 and each slot a `back` byte; every input is stamped with the render time its
  WebSocket frame would carry (`GenexFastTransport.RenderStamp`), so a lag-compensated shot sent by datagram is judged where it was aimed.
  A predicting fast seat keeps a listed body out of the keyframe while the newest snapshot lacks its record.
  Held to the `renderStamp`, `motionKnown` and regenerated uplink vectors.

## 0.4.0

- **Firstperson (PROTOCOL.md §17.7).** `GenexFirstperson.Step` (`Firstperson.cs`) ports the `firstperson` preset, Source-engine character movement through a static world of boxes and ramps, z up: friction with a stop-speed floor, ground and air acceleration (air-strafing), a free step up ledges, staying on slopes and stairs, ducking and crouch-jumping, a swept hull sliding along what it hits. The step filters each test through a bounding-volume tree over the solids (x, y and z) that only filters: candidates are visited in ascending index, and `GenexSmoke firstperson-diff` holds every result to brute force bit for bit (random worlds, stacked columns, map-wide solids, a 1500-box level, where a step costs about 13 µs through it against 260 µs without on .NET 8).
- `GenexAuthoritySession.SetWorld(world)` (and `GenexAuthorityOptions.World`): your entity in a `firstperson` room is predicted only while that world's hash equals the room's `params.world`, else drawn from the server's state without prediction, with `Prediction` (`{Predicting, Reason, World, RoomWorld}`, `Reason` `""`, `off`, `no_entity`, `no_preset` or `world_mismatch`) saying why. A new world re-seeds the prediction with the inputs in flight replayed on top. The prediction mirrors `pitch`, `duck`, `mstate` and (read only) `cap`; a respawn re-seeds them all. `GenexPresets.PredictionFor(text, world)`, `RoomWorld(text)` and `PitchLimit(text)` are public.
- `GenexAuthorityEntity` gains `Pitch` (yours predicted, others interpolated, held to the room's pitch limit), `Duck` (0 to 1), `Mstate` (yours predicted, `OnGround` 1, `Ducking` 2, `JumpHeld` 4), `Cap` and `ServerPitch`. `Telemetry` gains `PredictionSteps`, `PredictionStepMs` and `PeakFramePredictionMs`. A movement-bit or cap change is never counted as a correction.
- **The JSON reader is correctly rounded.** `GenexJson` no longer reads numbers with the BCL's `double.Parse`, which in Unity players (Mono and IL2CPP) is not correctly rounded: it read 160 of 3097 shortest-form numbers one ulp off, among them the float32 `-0.3616481423377991`, so `GenexFirstpersonWorld.FromJson` refused a world a JS exporter wrote that the server and .NET accept.
